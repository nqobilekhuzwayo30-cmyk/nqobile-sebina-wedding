Questions and Answers

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by March 1st, so we can have an accurate headcount.

Are kids welcome?

As much as we love your little ones, we will not be including them in the ceremony or reception.

Where should I park?

There is plenty of free parking available near the entrance of the venue.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The wedding talk and reception will both be held indoors.

Can we take pictures during the wedding?

Yes, photos and videos are welcome. Avoid photos during the wedding talk and do not post online without permission from the bride and groom.
FAQ

Can I bring extra guests?

Only guests who have RSVP'd are allowed to attend.

Can I bring my own alcohol?

No, outside alcohol is not permitted on the premises. Cars may be checked, and any drinks found in vehicles or concealed inside the venue will result in guests being asked to leave by Buena staff. The venue provides a fully stocked bar and refreshment service - please bring cash if you would like to purchase additional drinks beyond those served during the celebration.

Can I smoke or vape?

Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside the venue. Buena Vista reserves the right to issue warnings to anyone violating smoke-free zones, and may close the Banqueting Hall if guests continue to disregard this rule. Guests who fail to comply may also be asked to leave by Buena staff.

Can guests enter the kitchen?

No. The kitchen is reserved exclusively for caterers, waiters, and serving staff. Guests are not permitted to enter or linger in this area. Anyone found doing so will be asked to stop and return to enjoying the wedding celebrations.

What time should guests arrive?

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony.
🛡️ Guest Policy
We kindly remind all attendees that entry to the venue is restricted to guests who have received an official invitation and submitted their RSVP. Admission will only be granted to those listed on the RSVP with confirmed responses and assigned table numbers. For security reasons, anyone not on the RSVP list will not be permitted inside. Please do not bring additional guests or plus ones who are not registered.
🤝 Mutual Respect
Buena Vista holds the right to stop any function at any given time in the case that:
  • any of the guests at the function harass any of the Buena Vista staff members in any way;
  • guests obtain unauthorised access to the kitchen or any other unauthorised areas;
  • misbehaviour of guests and general breaking of Buena Vista rules.
