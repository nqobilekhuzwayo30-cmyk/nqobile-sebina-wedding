Questions & Answers

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by March 1st, so we can have an accurate headcount.

Are kids welcome?

As much as we love your little ones, they will not be included in the ceremony or reception.

Where should I park?

Free parking is available near the venue entrance.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony and reception will be held indoors.

Can we take pictures during the wedding?

Photos and videos are welcome. Please avoid photos during the ceremony and do not post online without permission.

FAQ

Can I bring my own alcohol?

No. Outside alcohol is not permitted. Cars may be checked. Any alcohol found may result in guests being asked to leave by Buena Vista staff.

The venue provides a fully stocked bar. Please bring cash if you wish to purchase additional drinks.

Can I smoke or vape?

Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside the venue. Buena Vista may issue warnings or ask guests to leave if rules are ignored.

Can guests enter the kitchen?

No. The kitchen is reserved for staff only. Guests entering this area will be asked to return to the celebration.

Mutual Respect

Buena Vista reserves the right to stop the function if staff are harassed, restricted areas are accessed, or venue rules are broken.

Guest Policy

Entry is limited to guests who received an official invitation and submitted an RSVP. Only confirmed guests with assigned table numbers will be allowed entry. Please do not bring unregistered guests or plus ones.
